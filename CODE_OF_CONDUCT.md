# Code of conduct

Nexus is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep discussions focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone
is representing the Nexus project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact method on their GitHub profile.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
